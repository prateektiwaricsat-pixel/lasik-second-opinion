1. Privacy policy

Who is responsible for your data

Dr. Prateek Tiwari, MBBS, MS (Ophthalmology), MP Medical Council Reg. No. 13522, Indore, Madhya Pradesh ("we", "us"). Contact for any privacy or data question: prateektiwaricsat@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your name, age, phone/WhatsApp number and email address.
  • The eye reports you upload (topography/tomography, pachymetry, prescriptions, the centre's recommendation and any other tests) and the information you share about your eyes and health.
  • Notes from your video consultation and the written report we prepare for you.
  • Payment confirmation details from our payment provider. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.
  • Basic, anonymous website usage data (pages visited, device type) if analytics is enabled on this site.

Why we use it

  • To review your reports and give you a specialist second opinion.
  • To schedule and conduct your video consultation and send you your written report.
  • To contact you about your booking, payment or report.
  • To keep a medical record of the consultation, as required for good medical practice.

We use your information only for your consultation. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with any LASIK centre, hospital or third party for marketing.

Consent

By submitting the form and uploading your reports, you consent to us processing your information for your consultation, as described here, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect anything already done before you withdrew.

Who else handles your data

We use trusted service providers only to run the service: an online form and file-storage provider (for your uploads), a payment gateway (for your fee), a scheduling tool (for your slot), video-call software, email and WhatsApp (to send messages and your report). Each receives only what it needs to perform its task. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Your reports, consultation notes and written report are kept securely for 3 years from your consultation, in line with standard medical-record practice in India, and then deleted. You can ask us to delete them earlier, unless we are legally required to keep them.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Write to prateektiwaricsat@gmail.com. We will acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including access-controlled accounts and secure (encrypted) connections. No online system is completely risk-free; if you have a concern, please contact us.

2. Refund & cancellation policy

The fee for the LASIK Second Opinion is ₹1,000. It covers review of your reports, a 20-minute video consultation and a signed written report. The fee is the same whatever the verdict.

  • Free reschedule: tell us at least 12 hours before your slot (on WhatsApp +91 99901 10596 or by email) and we will move it to another available slot at no cost.
  • Cancellation before the call: if you cancel before your video consultation takes place, you get a full refund.
  • If we cannot give an opinion: we check your reports within 24 hours of upload. If something is missing, we tell you which test to get and reschedule. If we still cannot give an opinion from the reports available, you get a full refund.
  • If we cancel: if we have to cancel your slot, you can choose a new slot or a full refund.
  • Missed call without notice: if you miss your slot without telling us 12 hours ahead, we will offer one rescheduled slot; further no-shows are not refundable.
  • After the call: once the video consultation has taken place, the fee is not refundable.

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval. To request a refund, write to prateektiwaricsat@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 99901 10596 with your name and payment details.

3. Terms of service

What this service is

A report-based specialist second opinion on refractive surgery (LASIK, SMILE, PRK/Trans-PRK, topography-guided treatments, ICL/IPCL), provided by Dr. Prateek Tiwari under India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020. It includes review of the reports you provide, a 20-minute video consultation and a signed written report (by email and WhatsApp, within 48 hours of the call).

What this service is not

  • It is not an in-person eye examination and does not replace one.
  • It is not surgical clearance. The final decision on any surgery is made by your operating surgeon after examining you in person.
  • It is not for emergencies. For sudden pain, loss of vision, injury or any eye emergency, go to the nearest eye hospital immediately.

Your responsibilities

  • Be at least 18 years old, or have a parent/guardian book and join the call.
  • Upload complete, genuine reports of your own eyes, and share accurate history. The opinion depends entirely on the information you provide.
  • Join the video call on time, from a quiet place with a stable connection.

Independence and disclosure

The fee is the same whatever the verdict. We receive no commission from any LASIK or laser centre, and you are free to get operated anywhere, including at your original centre. Dr. Prateek also performs eye surgery, including ICL. He will never suggest that you get operated by him; if you ask him yourself, it is at his standard rates, with no special offers.

Limits

The opinion is given in good faith, on the reports and information available at the time of consultation. Eye conditions can change, and tests can vary between machines and centres. We are not responsible for decisions made or outcomes of procedures performed by other surgeons or centres.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute is subject to the courts at Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Questions: prateektiwaricsat@gmail.com.
